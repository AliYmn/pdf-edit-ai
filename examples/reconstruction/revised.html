Q1 2024 Performance Summary

Prepared for: Yıldız Lojistik ve Ticaret A.Ş.  ·  Date: 19 April 2024

1. Overview

Northwind Analytics finished Q1 2024 ahead of plan: revenue rose in all three regions on the back of new logistics customers in Istanbul and Ankara, operating costs stayed within budget, and churn reached a record low.

3. Outlook

For the second quarter we expect continued growth as two enterprise contracts move from pilot to production. Hiring will focus on data engineering, and the next platform release will add carbon reporting for every delivery route.

In the third quarter Northwind will open a regional office in İzmir to serve customers along the Aegean coast. The office will start with a team of eight and handle onboarding and support for western Türkiye.

4. Risks

  • Fuel price volatility may compress customer margins and delay renewals.
  • Integration backlog with two ERP vendors could slow onboarding in Q2.
  • Currency movements (EUR/TRY) affect reported revenue from Turkish customers.

5. Next Steps

The leadership team will review the hiring plan in May and present the carbon-reporting roadmap to key customers in June. A detailed budget update will follow with the half-year report.
